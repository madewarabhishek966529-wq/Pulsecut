# Screen 1: Editor (most important, generate first)

Design the main editing screen of a web video editor called "Pulsecut".

Layout:
- Top bar (48px): logo "Pulsecut" with a small pulse icon, editable project title, undo/redo, aspect ratio dropdown (9:16, 16:9, 1:1), Export button (gradient, primary).
- Left panel (320px) with tabs: Media, Effects, Text, Audio. Show the Effects tab open: a search field, category chips (Glitch, Retro, Glow, Transitions, AI), and a 2-column grid of effect cards with thumbnails. At the top of the grid, a highlighted "Generate with AI" card with a sparkle icon.
- Center: a 9:16 video preview inside a dark stage with subtle grid, playback controls below (play, step back/forward, timecode "00:04:12 / 00:12:00" in mono, volume, fullscreen).
- Right panel (300px) Inspector: selected clip with sections Transform (position, scale, rotation sliders), Effects stack (Glitch with Intensity slider bound to "Bass" showing a small waveform badge, VHS with Grain slider), Opacity.
- Bottom timeline (280px): time ruler, a zoom slider, three tracks (Video with 3 clips and thumbnails, Text with 1 clip, Audio with waveform). Cyan playhead with glowing top handle and timecode chip. One clip selected with trim handles visible.

Mood: powerful but calm. Make it look like a real product, not a template.
